Our deep-dive workshop [**Interpretability in the lab and in the wild**](https://d3aconference.dk/interpretability-in-the-lab-and-in-the-wild/) at the [D3A conference](https://d3aconference.dk/) explored what happens when interpretability methods meet real data, real deadlines and real users. The room shared plenty of successes, failures and a few problems nobody has solved yet. Thanks to everyone who gave a talk or joined the discussions! Organised with Stella Frank, Teresa Dorszewski and Lorenz Linhardt.
