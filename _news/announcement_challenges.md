Our paper **Challenges in explaining deep learning models for data with biological variation** got published in [PLOS One](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0333965). The paper was created in collaboration with [FOSS Analytical A/S](https://www.fossanalytics.com/) and explores applying post-hoc explainability methods on biological data (specifically, images of grains), presents rarely discussed challenges and offers a framework for evaluation of the methods.
