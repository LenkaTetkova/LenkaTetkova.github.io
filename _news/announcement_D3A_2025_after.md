The [D3A conference](https://d3aconference.dk/) was eventful!
Our workshop [**Latent space navigation - interpretation, probing and steering**](https://d3aconference.dk/latent-space-navigation-interpretation-probing-and-steering/) was a big success.
Slides as well as hands-on Jupyter notebooks are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/LenkaTetkova/Latent-space-navigation)!
Moreover, our poster about the paper [From Colors to Classes: Emergence of Concepts in Vision Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.24071) won one of the **best poster awards**!
